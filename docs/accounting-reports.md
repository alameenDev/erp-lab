# Accounting dashboard

Open `/accounting-reports`. The default period is the current month. Apply the date, referral, payment status, operator, account/branch, collector, contract or patient filters. Click a lab/doctor/operator/contract/patient to open its invoices; click an invoice number to inspect charges, payments, package/group membership and recorded financial changes. The detailed-referral button lists all referring patients and their invoice balances. CSV and browser print/Save PDF export every matching record in the selected section, independently of table pagination.

## Financial definitions

- Only active (not soft-deleted) invoices are reported, dated by `invoices.created_at` in the application timezone. `registration_date` is available separately in invoice details/exports. Date boundaries are inclusive whole days.
- Invoice revenue is persisted `invoices.total`. Percentage discounts (type 2) are converted to amounts; other discounts are amounts. Loyalty discounts remain separate. `adjustment` exposes any difference between persisted total and subtotal less those discounts instead of silently recomputing historical invoices.
- Paid is the sum of non-deleted `invoice_paid_details.amount`, never the stale invoice `paid` column. Remaining = max(total − paid, 0), excess credit = max(paid − total, 0), calculated **per invoice** before summing. Paid/partial/unpaid/credit statuses are mutually exclusive.
- These are **current** balances for invoices in the selected creation period, not a historical balance as of the period end. Collections use payment creation dates in that period and can include older invoices. Other selected dimensions still apply to those invoices.
- The creator is the first recorded Invoice/User creation audit actor. When that does not exist, show the invoice account, or the referring portal account for `referral_request_uuid` invoices; label this historical fallback explicitly. Payment account is not asserted to be the actual collector.
- Estimated cost uses the catalogue's original cost (`tests.price`, `cultures.price`, `test_groups.original_price`) and stored package/group member cost when present. Missing snapshot metadata can be supplemented from the current catalogue. Package costs sum unique tests/cultures; `packages.price` is a sale price and is never treated as cost. Known zero is distinct from missing/null.
- Referral commission preserves the existing report's percentage convention, using the current active relationship for **that invoice's owning laboratory and explicit `referral_id_fk`** (doctor or other referrer). Missing/invalid rates are unknown. A separate `from_lab_id_fk` without an explicit commission referral does not create an invented commission charge. This is not a historical commission settlement ledger.
- Estimated profit = persisted invoice net − estimated test cost − estimated referral commission. Operational expenses, payroll, purchases, repeat-run costs and settlement payments are not recorded by this report. Therefore it is **not accounting net profit**. Any incomplete cost/commission makes the corresponding aggregate profit unknown; the coverage warning and known cost subtotal remain visible.
- Invoice net and commission are allocated to billed items by their persisted prices using integer largest remainders. Allocations reconcile exactly without multiplying package revenue by member counts. An invoice without stored items is shown as unallocated revenue.
- Analysis counts expand tests/cultures in groups and packages, deduplicate each leaf inside one billed container, and do not count result parameters as individual orders. Standalone and separately billed containers are separate orders. Legacy snapshots without complete membership use current relationships and are flagged. This is ordered analysis volume, not an instrument-run count.
- Referring lab balances are balances on their referred invoices. Outbound analysis reports describe destination and estimated cost; neither is proof of payments to a partner. The application lacks a complete outgoing partner-settlement ledger.

## Access and operational behavior

The existing `accounting reports view` permission applies, with administrator/lab-owner access as before. Partner/patient portals stay blocked. Every endpoint scopes invoices to the owning lab and staff descendants, including deleted staff history but excluding independent child labs. Options, payments, exports and direct invoice IDs use the same scope. Reports do not modify invoices, payments, balances or medical results. Detail audit output restricts changes to financial fields, not medical results or raw request data. Exports escape HTML and guard spreadsheet formulas. Responses are private and non-cacheable.

No new tables or data migration. Deploy backend and frontend together; the accounting API response now uses overview + paginated detail endpoints. Older accounting frontend bundles must be refreshed.

Automated coverage: `AccountingReportsTest` on MariaDB 10.11 and the actual Vue page browser fixture on desktop/mobile. The fixture uses synthetic records and makes no production data requests.
